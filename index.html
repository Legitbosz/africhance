How AfriChance works

  1. 1. We find it
    We track official government, NGO, foundation and university pages.
  2. 2. We verify it
    Official source, real deadline, clear eligibility. No source, no listing.
  3. 3. You get alerted
    Only for what matches your level, field and location, before it closes.
  4. 4. It disappears on time
    Expired offers are removed automatically. No dead links.

Why trust us

  • Official link on every post. If you can't check it on the organiser's own site, we don't post it.
  • Never pay to apply. Any offer asking you to pay to apply or to be shortlisted is a red flag. We don't list those.
  • Report a scam. See something wrong? Report it and we take it down and warn others.
  • No expired offers. Deadline is compulsory. Past deadline means gone, not still sitting there.
